Coil Me Maybe

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Coil Me Maybe is developed by Rafael Slivca. For privacy questions or support, contact a1z92f43as48r4gs5w8@outlook.com.

This policy covers the iOS game and its support website. The game is currently in development. App Store builds are configured to request live advertising, subject to your privacy choices, purchase status and Google’s ad-serving availability. Development and internal TestFlight builds use test ads.

Game progress and Game Center

The game stores settings, best height, earned spring finishes, challenge progress, selected reward targets and progress toward premium finishes from optional rewarded ads, and ad-frequency information on your device. It does not require an account with the developer or send gameplay records to a developer-operated server.

If you use Apple Game Center, the game sends your best height and completed challenge/ad-earned skin achievements to Game Center. It can restore your best height and these completed unlocks after reinstalling or changing devices. It also keeps local submission records associated with your Game Center player identifier. Apple handles Game Center account, leaderboard and achievement information; your Game Center identity, scores and achievements may be visible to other players through Game Center features. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Partial challenge and rewarded-ad progress, selected finishes and other settings stay on your device. Only completed challenge/ad-earned skin unlocks are synced as achievements when you connect through Pause → Leaderboard. Existing locally earned unlocks are associated with the first Game Center account that connects after this feature is installed. Unsynced unlocks and partial progress may be lost if local app data is removed. This feature uses Game Center, without requiring iCloud Drive or a developer-operated server. The Remove Ads purchase restores access to all premium finishes separately through StoreKit; purchase-only access is not recorded as an earned skin achievement.

Advertising

Ads can appear between runs. Separately, you can choose a premium finish and complete six optional rewarded ads to unlock it. Each completed ad adds one guaranteed step toward the chosen finish; closing before the reward is earned adds none. Progress is saved separately for each finish, and previously unlocked finishes stay owned. Height and challenge finishes are earned through gameplay.

The advertising integration uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform to deliver ads and manage privacy choices. When these services are used, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs and performance information. This information may be used for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and service improvement, and shared with advertising partners.

The game does not provide AdMob with your scores, spring finishes, Game Center identity or a custom player profile. It does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access for advertising. No additional mediation-network SDKs are included.

Depending on your location, permissions and choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. The advertising identifier is available only when permitted by Apple’s tracking rules. Google’s consent messages provide information about relevant partners and purposes.

See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. The game uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify purchase transactions and restore your entitlement, including after reinstalling or changing devices. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or operate a purchase-account server. After a verified purchase or restore, the game stops requesting and showing ads and unlocks all premium finishes. Achievement finishes remain tied to gameplay progress. Removing ads does not delete information already processed by advertising services. Apple manages payment, transaction records and refunds under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

When required, a privacy message asks for your choices before ads are requested. When available, open Settings → Ads & privacy → Manage privacy choices to change or withdraw choices, including applicable US sale/sharing opt-outs. Refusing consent or tracking permission does not prevent gameplay.

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app’s Ads & privacy screen also links to this policy and support. You can report an ad using its information or AdChoices button, or contact support with the advertiser name and a description of the issue.

Support and the website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send, and uses them to answer questions, investigate issues and handle privacy requests. Microsoft processes support email through Outlook under Microsoft’s privacy statement. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no added advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical visitor information, including IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and privacy requests

Local game data remains on your device until removed through the app or operating system. Removing the app does not necessarily delete Game Center records, support correspondence or data held by advertising services. The developer retains support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Apple, Google, Microsoft and GitHub describe retention of the information they process in their respective policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, withdraw consent, opt out of certain advertising uses, or complain to a data-protection authority. Email the support address above to make a request. We will explain what we can handle directly and how to reach the relevant provider for information it controls.

Children

Coil Me Maybe is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact support so we can investigate and address the request.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the game or its services change. The effective date identifies the current version; the latest policy will be available on this page.
